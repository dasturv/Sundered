Ancestries in Sundered represent the physiological advantages and disadvantages your character may have inherited from their ancestors. Unlike the races of other systems, Ancestries solely include the physical differences between each other; all cultural and behavioral traits are left up to you to include.

Attributes

Each Ancestry provides a starting rank for each Attribute, as well as a Natural Maximum. Natural Maximums represent a soft cap, after which improving said Attribute requires specialized training to push one's body, mind and spirit to their extremes. Natural Maximums are used during character creation and Advancement.

Example: You are playing as an Orc. Your Physique is shown to be 2/7. This means you have 2 ranks in PHY before spending any points, and have a Natural Maximum of 7.

Traits

Ancestries provide additional boons and flaws in the forms of Traits. You do not spend nor gain XP from Traits gained from your Ancestry, and cannot remove them through Advancement. You do not need to meet any requirements for Traits gained through your Ancestry.

Experience Points

Finally, your Ancestry provides your an amount of Experience Points (XP) that you can spend on Traits during character creation.
