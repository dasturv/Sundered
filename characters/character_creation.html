Characters in Sundered are not defined by classes nor levels. Instead, you build your character by choosing their Ancestry, Age, Background, Priorities, Attributes, Skills, Traits and other capabilities individually. To make a character, follow the steps below.

1. Choose an Ancestry

Choose your character's Ancestry.

Your Ancestry determines the starting values and Natural Maximums for your Attributes, alongside any number of innate abilities, advantages or disadvantages. Ancestries also provide a pool of Experience Points (XP) that can be spent later during character creation.

2. Choose an Age

Choose your character's Age.

Age represents your characters stage of life rather than a specific number of years. Your Ancestry determines the ages which it normally reaches for each category.

Your Age grants you a number of Experience Points to spend on Traits associated with your Background and may modify the maximums of some of your Attributes. Younger characters possess greater physical potential but have accumulated less experience, whereas an older character have greater access to Background Traits at the cost of physical, and eventually mental, decline.

Make sure to record any Attribute adjustments from your Age before moving on.

3. Choose a Background

Choose your character's Background.

Your Background represents your character's training, profession or way of life before becoming an adventurer. It determines both what Background Traits are available to you as well as your starting equipment when combined with your Resource Priority (see below).

4. Determine your Priorities

Assign A, B, C and D Priority between Attributes, Skills, Resources and Special. Each Priority can only be assigned once. Consult the table below when later steps call for it.

Priority Attributes Skills Resources Special
A 24 32 Exceptional 7
B 20 26 Established 5
C 16 20 Limited 3
D 12 14 Poor 1

Your choices determine where your character begins particularly strong and where they have made sacrifices.

5. Increase your Attributes

Spend the points granted by your Attributes Priority to increase your Physique, Finesse, Reflex, Endurance, Presence, Acumen, Awareness and Willpower on a one-for-one basis.

Only one Attribute can be raised to its Natural Maximum during character creation. All others must remain at least 1 below their Natural Maximum.

Attunement and Luck are Special Attributes and are not increased here.

6. Purchase Skills

Spend the points granted by your Skills Priority to increase your Skills on a one-for-one basis. All Skills start at rank 0.

Only one skill can begin at rank 6; all others must remain at or below rank 5 during character creation.

You may also purchase Specializations for 1 point each. Purchasing a Specialization requires at least 1 rank in it's parent Skill.

7. Increase your Special Attributes

Spend the points granted by your Special Priority to increase your Attunement and Luck on a one-for-one basis. Both begin at 0, and cannot be raised above their Natural Maximum during character creation, just like other Attributes.

A character does not need either Special Attribute. Completely mundane or not particularly lucky characters are perfectly valid.

8. Select Traits

Spend the XP provided by your Ancestry and Age on Traits. XP from your Ancestry can be spent on any Trait, whereas XP from your Age can only be spent on Traits listed in your Background. Unless otherwise stated, you cannot spend more than 15 XP on a single Trait during character creation.

You may also select Negative Traits to gain additional XP. You can gain up to 15 XP this way during character creation.

After purchasing Traits, you may keep up to 10 unspent XP, with any further unspent XP being lost.

9. Determine Resources

Consult your Background to determine your character's starting possessions and material circumstances. Your Background helps determine the kinds of equipment and assets appropriate to your character, while your Resource Priority determines their extent and quality.

Resources can include weapons, armor, adventuring equipment, professional tools, property, transportation, magical supplies, wealth, and more.

10. Final Touches

Record any remaining details needed to bring your character into the world, including their name, appearance, personality, relationships and motivations.

You should now have everything necessary to play your character.
