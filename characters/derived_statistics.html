Below are several statistics that are derived from Attributes, Skills and other parts of character creation.

Health and Overflow

A character's maximum Health is calculated by adding their Endurance and Base Health together.

Max Health = Base Health + END.

A creature's Base Health is decided by its size, as seen on the table below.

Size Base Health
Small 6
Medium 9
Large 15

Health Overflow is 1/2 of your Maximum Health, rounded down.

Initiative

A character's initiative is equal to rolling 2d6 and adding both its Reflex and Awareness Attributes.

Initiative = 2d6 + REF + AWR.

A Surprised character does not roll 2d6, instead only having REF + AWR for their Initiative.

Physical and Mental Limits

Each character has a built-in Limit for most Checks. Split between Physical and Mental, these Limits apply to any Check using an Attribute in one of those categories. Your Limit is equal to the average between your four associated Attributes (rounded down), plus two.

Physical Limit = [(PHY + FIN + REF + END) / 4] + 2

Mental Limit = [(PRE + ACU + AWR + WIL) / 4] + 2
