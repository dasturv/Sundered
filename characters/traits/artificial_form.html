Artificial Form

Innate. Your body was made, not born through natural means. This provides you several advantages and disadvantages:

  • You do not need to eat nor breathe. You still need to sleep.
  • Your lifestyle costs are reduced by 25%.
  • You are immune to poison and disease.
  • When you recieve magical healing, it is considered to be two Force lower than what is was cast at for the purposes of you recovering. This does not reduce Drain nor any other effects of the spell.
  • You cannot gain benefits from potions or other items that need to be consumed.
  • First Aid done on you uses an appropriate Crafting Skill instead of Medicine.
