Small

Innate. You are Small. This provides many advantages and disadvantages:

  • Your base health is reduced to 6 before applying your END.
  • Your carrying capacity is halved.
  • Your move speed is reduced by 1.
  • Your Reach is reduced by 1, to a minimum of 0.
  • You gain +1 dice on Dodge and Stealth Checks.
  • Your lifestyle costs and cost of armor are reduced by 25%.
