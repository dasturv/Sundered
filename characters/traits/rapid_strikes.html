Rapid Strikes

Requirements: FIN 3
XP Cost: 15 XP
Max Rank: I

Maneuver. You have learned how to trade accuracy for speed. Whenever you take the Attack action using a weapon, you may gain 1 Exertion to make two attacks instead of one. When you do so, you suffer a -2 dice penalty to both attacks. You still suffer penalties for repeated attacks as normal.
