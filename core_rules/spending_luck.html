Unlike the other Attributes, Luck is very rarely used in Checks. Instead, they grant the ability to alter fate in the form of Luck Points.

Luck Points

A character has access to a number of Luck Points equal to their Luck Attribute. These can be spent in a number of different ways. A character recovers one expended Luck Point each time they complete a Rest. A character can never have more Luck Points than their Luck Attribute.

Spending Luck

There are several ways to spend Luck Points, and even your Luck Attribute itself, as seen below.

  • Stroke of Luck. After making a Check, you may spend 1 Luck Point to reroll any number of the d6s, potentially increasing your number of Hits. You can use this only once per Check or Extended Check.
  • Avoid Misfortune. When you would suffer a Mishap, you may spend 1 Luck Point to avoid the consequences of the Mishap. You still fail the Check.
  • Cheat Death. When you would die, you may permanently reduce your Luck Attribute by one to instead not die. You become Stabilized, but you still suffer all other consequences of your injuries.
