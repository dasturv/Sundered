Attributes represent a character's innate capabilities, and are used in most Checks. They are seperated into Physical, Mental and Special Attributes.

Physical Attributes

Physique (PHY)

Physique represents raw physical power. It governs a character's ability to lift, push, grapple, break and otherwise exert force. Physique also important in getting the most from many weapons and resisting the effects of Exertion.

Finesse (FIN)

Finesse represents coordination, precision and fine motor control. It governs actions requiring careful or accurate movement and contributes to the use of precise weapons.

Reflex (REF)

Reflex represents reaction and the speed at which a character can respond to danger. It is especially important for avoiding attacks and acting quickly in combat.

Endurance (END)

Endurance represents toughness, stamina and physical resilience. It governs resistance to injury, exhaustion, disease and poisons.

Mental Attributes

Presence (PRE)

Presence represents confidence, creativity and force of personality. It governs a character's ability to influence others socially as well as communicate effectively.

Acumen (ACU)

Acumen represents reasoning, memory and the ability to understand complex subjects. It is important for scholarly pursuits, technical knowledge, crafting and investigation.

Awareness (AWR)

Awareness represents perception, intuition and attentiveness to the surrounding world. It governs gut feelings, spotting hidden details, navigation and reading body language.

Willpower (WIL)

Willpower represents resolve, discipline and self-control. It governs a character's ability to resist fear, coercion and supernatural influence.

Special Attributes

Attunement (ATT)

Attunement represents a character's capacity to channel and control supernatural power. It governs the maximum power of spells one can cast and maintain. See Spellcasting for more details.

Luck (LCK)

Luck represents those moments when fortune turns in a character's favor. Unlike other Attributes, Luck is not normally added to checks. See Spending Luck for more details.

Most non-player characters have 0 Luck, with only legendary foes or important NPCs having access to Luck.
