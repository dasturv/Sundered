Skills represents a character's training, knowledge and practiced abilities. When making a Check, a Skill is usually combined with an appropriate Attribute to form a Dice Pool.

The Attribute used alongside a Skill depends on how the action is being performed. For example, PRE + Intimidation may be used to threaten someone, whereas PHY + Intimidation may be used to display physical dominance.

Specializations

Specializations represent focused training within a broader Skill. Each Specialization is associated with a particular Skill and applies only when its specific area of expertise is relevant. You cannot have a Specialization in a Skill you have no Ranks in.

When making a Check to which one or more of your Specializations applies, you gain +2 dice.

Example: You have Blades 3 (Daggers). You would gain +2 dice when attacking with a dagger, but not with a longsword.

Specializations should represent a narrower field of expertise rather than an activity covered by the entire Skill. The Specializations listed for each Skill are examples rather than an exhaustive list.

Untrained Skills

Some Skills are more difficult to use without proper training. These are marked Untrained (−2). When making a Check using one of these Skills while you have 0 Ranks in it, you suffer −2 dice. Other Skills can be used at 0 Ranks without penalty.

List of Skills

Acrobatics

Training in balance, tumbling, controlled falls, and precise bodily movement.

Specializations: Balance, Contortion, Falling

Animal Handling

Untrained (-2) Training in calming, training, riding and caring for animals.

Specializations: Husbandry, Riding, Taming, Training

Athletics

Training in strenuous physical movement and feats of athletic ability.

Specializations: Climbing, Jumping, Running, Swimming

Axes

Untrained (-2) Training with chopping and hooked weapons, from small hatchets to heavy battleaxes.

Specializations: Battleaxes, Greataxes, Handaxes

Blades

Untrained (-2) Training with knives and swords of all sizes, emphasizing the use of an edged or pointed blade.

Specializations: Daggers, Longsword, Rapiers

Brawling

Untrained (-2) Training in the use of unarmed strikes, grappling, wrestling and improvised weapons.

Specializations: Improvised Weapons, Unarmed Strikes, Grappling

Clubs

Untrained (-2) Training with impact weapons designed to bludgeon or strike with heavy force.

Specializations: Clubs, Maces, Whips

Deception

Training in misleading others through lies, misdirection, disguise and manipulation.

Specializations: Lying, Impersonation, Disguise

Dodge

Training in quickly avoiding attacks, hazards, and other immediate dangers.

Specializations: Melee, Projectiles, Magic

Hammers

Untrained (-2) Training with weapons whose power comes from concentrated heavy force.

Specializations: Mauls, Light Hammers, Lucernes

Influence

Training in changing the opinions or behavior of others through persuasion and negotiation.

Specializations: Diplomacy, Bartering, Seduction

Insight

Training in interpreting behavior, emotions, intentions, and subtle social cues.

Specializations: Deception, Emotions, Motives

Intimidation

Training in influencing others through threats, fear, and displays of dominance.

Specializations: Interrogation, Physical Dominance, Threats

Investigation

Training in methodical searching, deduction, and drawing conclusions from available evidence.

Specializations: Crime Scenes, Research, Reasoning

Leadership

Untrained (-2) Training in directing, organizing, teaching and inspiring others.

Specializations: Command, Coordination, Teaching

Marksmanship

Untrained (-2) Training in ranged weapons, including bows, crossbows, slings, and similar projectile weapons.

Specializations: Bows, Crossbows, Blowguns

Medicine

Untrained (-2) Training in diagnosing and treating injuries, diseases, and other physical ailments.

Specializations: Disease, First Aid, Surgery, Toxins

Perception

Training in noticing details and detecting things through the senses.

Specializations: Hearing, Sight, Smell

Performance

Untrained (-2) Training in entertaining or captivating an audience through artistic expression.

Specializations: Acting, Dance, Music, Oratory, Singing

Polearms

Untrained (-2) Training with long-hafted weapons used for reach, thrusting, sweeping attacks, and controlling distance.

Specializations: Glaives, Halberds, Spears

Shields

Untrained (-2) Training in the active use of shields to intercept and deflect incoming attacks.

Specializations: Bucklers, Round Shields, Tower Shields

Sleight of Hand

Training in subtle hand movements, manual tricks, and manipulating objects without being noticed.

Specializations: Concealment, Pickpocketing, Parlor Tricks

Spellcraft

Untrained (-2) Training in the practicial manipulation and control of magic, including casting and modifying spells. Magical traditions cannot be chosen as Spellcraft Specializations.

Specializations: Countermagic, Spell Attacks, Rituals

Stealth

Training in moving, hiding, and acting without being detected.

Specializations: Camouflage, Shadowing, Sneaking

Survival

Untrained (-2) Training in navigating and surviving dangerous or uncivilized environments.

Specializations: Foraging, Navigation, Tracking, Trapping

Throwing

Training in throwing weapons and weapons designed to enhance the natural throwing motions.

Specializations: Javelins, Slings, Throwing Knives

Education Skills

Some Skills come about from training in a particular trade, topic, or occupation. Education Skills come in one of three categories: Craft Skills, Profession Skills and Lore Skills. Each Education Skill is learned and advanced seperately.

The lists of Education Skills are intentionally open-ended. Players and GMs may introduce additional Skills appropriate to a character's background and the setting. You can also still gain Specializations in Education Skills.

All Education Skills have Untrained (-2).

Craft Skills

Craft Skills cover the creation, repair, and evaluation of particular types of goods, such as Smithing, Carpentry, Masonry, Tailoring or Cooking. Enchanting and Alchemy are both Craft Skills.

Profession Skills

Profession Skills cover the specialized knowledge and practical experience of particular occupations, such as Sailor, Farmer, Miner, Merchant or Thief.

Lore Skills

Lore Skills cover the formal education of specific topics, such as History, Culture, Natural World, Linguistics and Arcana.
