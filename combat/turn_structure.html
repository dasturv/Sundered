Combat in Sundered is seperated into Rounds and Turns, during which each character decides what to do and how to react to one another.

Actions and AP

Actions are, simply put, the things a character can do. Each Action has its own effects and costs a certain amount of Action Points (AP). Generally, you have 3 AP that you recover at the start of each of your Turns.

Reactions

While most Actions are taken on your Turn, there are some that can occur out of Turn order. Reactions are special Actions that have a condition, that when triggered, allows you to take the Reaction immediately. When a character takes a Reaction, the normal flow of Combat stops until the Reaction concludes, then the current Turn continues. If multiple characters trigger a Reaction at the same time, they are resolved in Turn order.

Initiative

At the start of Combat, each character rolls their Initiative. A character rolls 2d6 and adds their REF and AWR to the total to get their Initiative. The GM then compares each character's Initiative to decide the order of play, starting with the highest total. When one or more characters tie on their Initiative roll, player characters go before non-player characters, players decide who goes first when multiple of their characters tie, and the GM decides who goes first when multiple non-player characters tie.

When Initiative is rolled, each character gains 1 Action Point (AP).

Ambushes

Sometimes a character isn't ready for battle. When a character is Surprised, they do not roll 2d6 for their Initiative, relying solely on their REF and AWR. Additionally, a Surprised character gains no AP when Initiative is rolled.

Taking your Turn

When it eventually becomes your Turn, you follow these steps:

  1. Resolve anything that occurs at "start of turn"
  2. You lose any unspent AP, then gain 3 AP
  3. You take Actions, spend AP and do whatever else you can on your Turn
  4. You declare your Turn ended
  5. Resolve anything that occurs at "end of turn"

To see what Actions are available to you, see Basic Actions for a list of Actions available to all characters.
