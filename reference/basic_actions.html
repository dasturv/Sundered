Attack — 1 AP

You make an attack with a weapon you are wielding. See Attacking for more details.

Block — 1 AP

As a Reaction when you would be hit by an attack, you attempt to block with a weapon or shield you are wielding. See Blocking for more details.

Dash — 1 AP

You move up to 1.5x your speed (rounded down), but gain 1 Exertion. See Movement for more details.

Hide — 1 AP

You make a FIN + Stealth Check opposed by your targets' AWR + Perception Check. If the target is Aware of you and you aren't already Hidden, you suffer a -4 dice penalty on this Check. On a success, you become Hidden.

Move — 1 AP

You move up to your speed. See Movement for more details.
