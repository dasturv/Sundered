Sundered uses many different damage types, each representing a unique way to cause harm.

Physical Damage

Physical damage types are those which are dealt by weapons and manual force. Physical damage is most often resisted through armor and Endurance.

  • Blunt. Damage caused by crushing force, impacts and heavy blows.
  • Pierce. Damage caused by thrusts, spikes and stabs.
  • Slash. Damage caused by cuts, slicing edges and chopping.

Elemental Damage

Elemental damage types are those caused by naturally occuring sources of high (or low) energy. Elemental damage is not often resisted by mundane armor, but can be resisted with Endurance.

  • Acid. Damage caused by corrosion or chemical reaction.
  • Cold. Damage caused by freezing temperatures.
  • Fire. Damage caused by burning or high temperatures.
  • Lightning. Damage caused by electricity.
  • Sonic. Damage caused by shockwaves and dangerously loud sounds

Esoteric Damage

Esoteric damage types are those caused by supernatural forces. Esoteric damage is not often resisted by armor, and cannot be resisted with Endurance.

  • Holy. Damage caused by divine power.
  • Magic. Damage caused by distilled arcane energy.
  • Occult. Damage caused by demonic or spiritual power.
  • Psychic. Damage caused by psionic power and mental strain.
