Exertion

Exertion is a measure of how hard you are pushing your body. Certain actions or features can increase Exertion. Whenever you start your turn with one or more Exertion, you must make a PHY Check, with each Hit reducing your Exertion by 1. You then suffer Stun equal to any remaining Exertion, and the Extertion is removed.

Example: You are in an intense fight with a group of bandits. On your turn you choose to Dash with one of your actions, gaining 1 Exertion. Later that round you manage to block a bandit's attack, using your Parry trait to counterattack at the cost of 2 more Extertion. At the start of your next turn you have 3 Exertion, and score 2 Hits on your PHY Check. You suffer 1 Stun and your Exertion is reset.
