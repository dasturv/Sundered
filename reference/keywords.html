Exertion

Exertion is a measure of how hard you are pushing your body. Certain actions or features can increase Exertion. Whenever you start your turn with one or more Exertion, you must make a PHY Check, with each Hit reducing your Exertion by 1. You then suffer Stun equal to any remaining Exertion, and the Extertion is removed.

Example: You are in an intense fight with a group of bandits. On your turn you choose to Dash with one of your actions, gaining 1 Exertion. Later that round you manage to block a bandit's attack, using your Parry trait to counterattack at the cost of 2 more Extertion. At the start of your next turn you have 3 Exertion, and score 2 Hits on your PHY Check. You suffer 1 Stun and your Exertion is reset.

Hidden

A character is Hidden from another when their target has not or cannot accurately pinpoint their location. This does not always mean through sight, provided the target has another sense that can detect the exact location of the character.

A character suffers a -2 dice penalty on Dodge Checks against attacks from Hidden characters, though attacking while Hidden reveals your position unless you immediately take the Hide action to remain Hidden. If you do not Hide or you fail the opposing Check, you are no longer Hidden.

Prone

A Prone character has the qualities:

  • Their speed is halved (rounded down).
  • Their Attack rolls using melee weapons suffer a dice penalty equal to the weapon's Reach.
  • Their Attack rolls using ranged weapons suffer a -4 dice penalty.
  • They suffer a -2 dice penalty on Dodge Checks against melee weapons.
  • Attacks using ranged weapons targeting them suffer a -2 dice penalty.
  • They gain a +2 dice bonus on Stealth Checks to remain Hidden.

A character can drop Prone on their turn as a free action. A Prone character can stand up on their turn for 1 AP.

Surprised

A character is considered Surprised when they are Unaware of all threats when Combat begins. Threats do not necessarily need to be Hidden from the character, as long as they are not expecting any hostile action.

Example: You and your friend are walking down the crowded streets of a city, when all of a sudden the figure in front of you wheels around, knife in hand. Even though you could see the attacker, you weren't expecting to get mugged in a crowd, so you are Surprised and don't add 2d6 to your Initiative. Your friend, however, managed to see the mugger draw the knife, and so is not Surprised.

Unaware

A character is Unaware of another when they don't register them as a threat. This can either mean the target is not aware the attacker is there, or they do not see them as a threat.

Attacking an Unaware character imposes a -2 dice penalty on their Dodge Check. Regardless of if you hit or miss, the target becomes Aware of the threat.
